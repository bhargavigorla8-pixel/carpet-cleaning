How to Remove Tough Stains from Your Sofa: A Complete Upholstery Cleaning Guide

Learn how professional upholstery cleaning helps remove stubborn stains, refresh fabric, and keep your sofa looking clean, comfortable, and fresh.

Clean Nest Team Cleaning Experts
September 21, 2026
7 min read

Your sofa is one of the most frequently used pieces of furniture in your home. Over time, dust, food particles, body oils, pet hair, spills, and everyday dirt can become trapped deep inside the fabric.

While regular vacuuming helps maintain the surface, professional upholstery cleaning can provide a much deeper clean and help restore the freshness of your furniture.

WHY IT MATTERS

Why Sofa Cleaning Matters

Sofas collect more dirt than many people realize. Everyday use can cause dust, stains, hair, and other particles to build up between cushions and inside upholstery fibers.

Without regular cleaning, your sofa may gradually lose its fresh appearance and develop unwanted odors. Professional upholstery cleaning helps clean areas that normal surface cleaning cannot reach.

Cleaning Tip

Treat spills as soon as possible. The longer a stain remains on upholstery fabric, the more difficult it can become to remove.

COMMON PROBLEMS

Common Sofa Stains

Different types of spills and stains can affect upholstery in different ways. Some of the most common problems include:

Coffee & Tea Stains

Drinks can leave visible marks and may penetrate deep into fabric fibers.

Food & Oil Marks

Grease and food particles can create stubborn spots on upholstery.

Pet Hair & Dirt

Pet hair, dust, and outdoor dirt can accumulate deep inside sofa fabric.

Liquid Spills

Water, juice, soft drinks, and other liquids can leave noticeable marks.

PROFESSIONAL CARE

CLEANING FREQUENCY

How Often Should You Clean Your Sofa?

The ideal cleaning frequency depends on how frequently your furniture is used and the conditions inside your home.

Homes with children or pets may require more frequent upholstery cleaning, while lightly used furniture may require less frequent professional attention.

Usage
Suggested Cleaning

As needed
FINAL THOUGHTS

Give Your Sofa a Fresh Start

Regular care can help keep your sofa looking fresh and comfortable for longer. Vacuuming, quickly addressing spills, and arranging professional cleaning when needed can all contribute to better upholstery care.

If your sofa has accumulated stains, dust, pet hair, or unwanted odors, professional upholstery cleaning can provide a deeper refresh than everyday cleaning methods.
