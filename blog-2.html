How Often Should You Professionally Clean Your Carpets?

Discover how regular professional carpet cleaning can improve your home's appearance, comfort, freshness and overall indoor environment.

Sarah Wilson Cleaning Expert
September 21, 2026
6 Min Read

Your carpets do more than add comfort and warmth to your home. They also collect dust, dirt, allergens, pet hair and everyday debris. While regular vacuuming is important, it cannot remove everything trapped deep inside carpet fibers.

Professional carpet cleaning helps remove deeply embedded dirt and buildup that ordinary household cleaning may leave behind. But how often should you actually schedule a professional cleaning?

How Often Should You Clean Your Carpets?

For most homes, professional carpet cleaning every 6 to 12 months is a good starting point. However, the ideal cleaning schedule depends on your lifestyle, household size, pets and the amount of traffic your carpets receive.

Quick Tip

High-traffic homes may benefit from professional carpet cleaning more frequently than homes with lighter foot traffic.

5 Signs Your Carpet Needs Professional Cleaning

Sometimes your carpet can look clean while dirt and residue are still hidden deep within the fibers. Watch for these common signs.

01

Visible Stains

Persistent stains that remain after regular cleaning may require professional treatment.

02

Unpleasant Odors

Lingering smells can come from dirt, spills, pets or moisture trapped deep inside carpet fibers.

03

Heavy Foot Traffic

Busy hallways, living rooms and entryways can accumulate dirt much faster than other areas.

04

Flattened Carpet

High-traffic areas can become flattened and dull over time due to accumulated dirt and pressure.

05

It Has Been Over a Year

If you cannot remember the last time your carpets were professionally cleaned, it may be time to schedule a service.

Why Regular Professional Cleaning Matters

Regular professional cleaning is about more than simply making your carpet look fresh. Dirt particles can become embedded deep inside carpet fibers and gradually affect their appearance and texture.

Professional cleaning can help refresh the fibers, remove stubborn buildup and restore the appearance of heavily used areas.

Fresh Appearance

Restore a cleaner and fresher appearance to your carpets.

Cleaner Environment

Remove accumulated dirt and unwanted particles from carpet fibers.

More Comfortable Home

Enjoy softer, cleaner and more comfortable living spaces.

Better Carpet Care

Regular maintenance helps keep carpets looking their best.

Don't Forget About Upholstery

Your sofas, chairs and other upholstered furniture also collect dust, food particles, pet hair and everyday dirt. Just like carpets, upholstery can benefit from professional cleaning.

Depending on how frequently furniture is used, professional upholstery cleaning every 6 to 12 months can help keep it looking fresh and inviting.

"A clean carpet doesn't just change the way your home looks — it can change the way your home feels."

Simple Ways to Keep Your Carpet Cleaner

  • Vacuum regularly, especially in high-traffic areas.
  • Clean spills as soon as possible.
  • Use entrance mats to reduce tracked-in dirt.
  • Avoid excessive moisture when cleaning carpets.
  • Schedule professional cleaning regularly.

Give Your Carpets the Care They Deserve

Regular vacuuming and quick spot cleaning are important parts of carpet care, but professional cleaning can provide a deeper refresh.

If your carpets look tired, smell unpleasant or have not been professionally cleaned for a long time, it may be the perfect time to give them some professional attention.
