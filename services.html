QUESTIONS

Cleaning FAQs

Most standard carpet cleaning appointments take around 1–2 hours depending on the size, condition and number of areas being cleaned.

Drying time depends on fabric, ventilation and humidity. Most upholstery is ready for normal use within several hours.

We can treat many common stains, but results depend on the stain type, age and fabric. Our technician will assess it before cleaning.

We can move lightweight furniture when required. Heavy or fragile furniture may need to remain in place for safety.

We use cleaning solutions selected according to the material and cleaning requirement. Please inform us about any specific sensitivities before the appointment.
